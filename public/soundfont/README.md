GeneralUser GS v2.0.3 by S. Christian Collins

A GM and GS compatible SoundFont bank, used by DodoTabs as the alphaTab playback
soundfont (`GeneralUser-GS.sf2`). 261 instrument presets, 13 drum kits, ~30 MB.
